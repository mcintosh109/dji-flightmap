    How to update

    1. Open the Proxmox web page, click your server name on the left, then >_ Shell.
    2. Paste this and press Enter (in the Shell, paste with Ctrl+Shift+V):
    3. Wait until it says "Update finished. The map is working".
    4. Come back to this page and press Ctrl+Shift+R.

    Your settings, map keys and login are saved before the update and put back afterwards. If the new version doesn't start, the update undoes itself.
