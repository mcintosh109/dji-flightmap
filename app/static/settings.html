    Placeholder slots
    
          
    Up to 4 routes can be on the RC 2 at once, each in its own slot: a placeholder mission
            you make in DJI Fly. The number of waypoints tells them apart: 2 waypoints = slot 1, 3 = slot 2, 4 = slot 3,
            5 = slot 4 (put them anywhere, they are never flown). Confirm each one once below; after that the map
            remembers it, whatever route it holds. Only these 4 missions can ever be replaced.

          Connection
          
      
            
            
            
          
      
          
      Private certificate (recommended): the map makes its own. No domain needed. Each device imports it once (steps below), then shows the padlock.
      
          
      Let's Encrypt: a free certificate every device trusts straight away, for a name under a domain you own (or a free duckdns.org name). Renews itself.
      
          
      Off: everything, including your password, travels unencrypted on your network.
